No account or login is needed. The app reads public news, events and newsletter data from nordiccrypto.no.

Apple TV: the Today tab is an information screen that rotates on its own. Play/Pause turns the background radio (Radio Norge) on or off. Radio is played live from the broadcasters' own public streams and is not stored; FluxFM's public streams can also play.

The Nordic Shield NFT tab shows a QR code for Wally Wallet on the viewer's phone. The badge is a free collectible with no economic rights; there are no in-app purchases and the app handles no payments or keys.
